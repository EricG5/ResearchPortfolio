Robotic Characterization of Markerless Hand-Tracking on Meta Quest Pro and Quest 3

Eric Godden, William Steedman, & Matthew Pan — March 7, 2025

PDF Code BibTeX

Abstract

We propose an evaluation methodology leveraging a robotic manipulator to measure positional accuracy, jitter, and latency, providing a standardized characterization framework for markerless hand-tracking. We apply this to Meta Quest Pro and Quest 3 devices.
