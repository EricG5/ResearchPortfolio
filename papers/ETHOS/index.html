ETHOS: A Robotic Encountered-Type Haptic Display for Social Interaction in VR

Eric Godden, Jacquie Groenewegen, & Matthew Pan — ICRA 2026 (in review)

PDF Code BibTeX

Abstract

ETHOS (Encountered-Type Haptics for On-demand Social interaction) is a dynamic encountered-type haptic display that enables natural physical contact in VR for social interactions such as handovers, fist bumps, and high-fives, using a torque-controlled robot and interchangeable props.
